## 1. Donor / sample independence — RESOLVED

**Question.** Do the 20 samples used for patient-level inference correspond to independent donors?

**Method.** GEO SOFT series metadata, NCBI BioSample records via E-utilities, and the full text of the source publications.

**GSE156405.** The source paper is explicit:

> "A total of **9 patients** were recruited at the University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center through informed written consent following institutional review board approval (Lab00–396 and PA15–0014)."
>
> "Five primary samples (P1–P5) were obtained via EUS-FNAs of the pancreas, while four metastatic samples were obtained as core needle biopsies (liver, lung) or segmental biopsies (vaginal apex, peritoneal)."
>
> "we performed scRNA-seq on PBMCs from **8 of 9 patients** obtained at or around the time of their tissue biopsies."

This explains the 9 tumour samples and the 8 PBMC samples, and it settles the LiM/LuM/PM/VM question: they are **four separate patients**, not metastases from P1–P5.

**GSE155698.** Five used samples (GSM4710689–4710693, `PDAC_TISSUE_1`–`5`) carry five distinct BioSamples and no same-patient annotation. The series does contain one documented same-patient pair (`GSM4710699`/`GSM4710700`, "Sample 11A and 11B are from the same patient, processed at two separate core facilities") — **neither is used here**.

**GSE212966.** Six used samples carry six distinct BioSamples; the series describes "six PDAC and six adjacent noncancerous resection specimens".

**Conclusion.** 20 samples, 20 donors. The dataset-stratified patient bootstrap does not double-count donors. NCBI BioSample records carry no donor field for any of the three series, so the source-paper text is the binding evidence; this is recorded rather than assumed.

### 1a. New precision item: treatment status is largely recoverable

The manuscript currently states:

> "Treatment status was not consistently available across the public single-cell and bulk sources…" (L238)

That understates what is actually documented:

| Series | Documented status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| GSE155698 (5 samples) | **Treatment naïve** | SOFT `characteristics_ch1: treatment: Treatment Naïve` |
| GSE156405 P1–P5 (5 samples) | Not stated for the primary biopsies | — |
| GSE156405 VM | **Post-treatment**: 5-fluorouracil / liposomal irinotecan | source paper, Results |
| GSE156405 LuM | **Post-treatment**: evofosfamide / ipilimumab | source paper, Results |
| GSE156405 PM | **Post-treatment**: capecitabine | source paper, Results |
| GSE212966 (6 samples) | **No treatment** | SOFT `characteristics_ch1: treatment: No treatment` |

So of the 20 samples, **17 are documented treatment-naïve and 3 (VM, LuM, PM) are documented post-treatment**. The accurate statement is not "unavailable" but "recoverable at the single-cell level and not consistently available for the bulk cohorts". Two of the three post-treatment samples are in the P1-A1 and P1-A2 estimable sets, so the qualifier matters.

## 2. TREM2_TAM semantics — project-defined construct

**Finding.** The nine-class stratification is generated by `scripts/tasks/_task5_myeloid_subtypes.py` (created 2026-06-06), which scores nine hand-specified marker modules with `scanpy.tl.score_genes` and assigns each cell by `idxmax`. The TREM2_TAM module is `TREM2, CD68, CX3CR1, APOE, SPP1`.

It is **not** a subtype published by Derosiers et al. or by any of the three source studies, and it is not derived from any published clustering. It is a deterministic, project-defined marker-module construct.

**Consequence for wording.** The manuscript currently says (L93):

> "Fine myeloid enrichment used a **pre-existing** algorithmic nine-class stratification of the same three series…"

"Pre-existing" is true relative to S24 (June precedes July), but a reviewer can read it as "published by the source study". That reading would be wrong and would invite an unnecessary challenge.

**Recommended wording.**

> "…a deterministic marker-module stratification of the same three series, defined before the cross-scale analysis and containing neither the target gene nor the outcome genes…"

The supplementary material already uses the appropriate hedge ("algorithmic", "not externally validated") and needs no change.

## 3. Estimability selection — associated with yield, not detectably with target abundance

**13 estimable / 7 non-estimable**, and the seven are not homogeneous:

| Missing reason | n | Samples |
|---|---|---|
| `too_few_TREM2_cells` (<10) | 4 | PT3 (5 cells), P1 (1), P2 (4), P5 (6) |
| `too_few_SIGLEC15pos_TREM2_cells` (<2) | 3 | LuM (283 TREM2 cells, **0 positive**), PT1 (1 positive), PM (1 positive) |

Note that LuM has the second-largest TREM2 compartment in the whole collection and still cannot be estimated, because it contains no SIGLEC15-positive TREM2 cell at all. The two missingness mechanisms are therefore genuinely different and should not be pooled in a single sentence.

**Comparison, estimable vs non-estimable (Mann–Whitney):**

| Variable | Estimable median | Non-estimable median | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| included myeloid cells | 521 | 218 | **0.0005** |
| median total counts | 9,272 | 4,644 | 0.056 |
| **SIGLEC15-positive fraction A_i** | **0.01495** | **0.01556** | **0.817** |
| TREM2 fraction O_i | 0.266 | 0.085 | 0.351 |
| TREM2 cells | 141 | 6 | 0.037 |

**Reading.** Estimability was strongly associated with myeloid-cell yield, which is the mechanical requirement of the eligibility rule. **We found no evidence that estimability selected samples with higher SIGLEC15-positive-cell prevalence** (A_i 0.01495 vs 0.01556; p = 0.82). This is an absence of a detected difference at n = 20, **not** a demonstration that the two groups are equivalent on target abundance. **TREM2_TAM-like occupancy differed numerically (0.266 vs 0.085) and remained imprecisely estimated** (p = 0.35); that difference was not resolved in either direction and must not be reported as absent.

The defensible statement is therefore narrow: the within-state contrast is not conditioned on a high-abundance subset of tumours, and the estimable set was defined by compartment resolvability rather than by target prevalence. The restriction still means the contrast describes the 13 tumours with a resolvable TREM2 compartment, not all 20, and that sentence must appear.

## 4. ACP5 vs MMP9 within TREM2_TAM — no detectable predominance at this layer

Computed only as a claim audit: the same within-sample whole-myeloid z-scores, the same 13 primary-eligible patients, no change to the primary outcome.

| Quantity | mean | 95% CI | positive direction | median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K<sub>i,T</sub> **ACP5** | 0.865 | 0.487–1.384 | **13/13** | 0.507 |
| K<sub>i,T</sub> **MMP9** | 1.128 | 0.543–1.789 | 11/13 | 0.755 |
| K<sub>i,T</sub> core (ACP5+MMP9) | 0.996 | 0.559–1.550 | 13/13 | 0.575 |

**Paired comparison, MMP9 − ACP5, same 13 patients:** mean +0.263, median −0.141, **Wilcoxon p = 0.74**, MMP9 larger in only **6 of 13**.

**Reading.** Inside TREM2_TAM, ACP5 does **not** attenuate: it is positive in every estimable patient. The paired MMP9−ACP5 difference was not detectably different from zero (p = 0.74), with MMP9 larger in only 6 of 13 patients; the mean and median of that difference have opposite signs, which is the signature of a skewed few-patient tail rather than a consistent shift. **This is a failure to detect systematic MMP9 predominance, not a demonstration of equivalence** — the paired difference rests on 13 patients with 2–24 positive cells each, so a moderate predominance is not excluded.

What is displaced is the claim that **MMP9 predominance is a stable property of the whole construct**. What is **not** displaced is the original observation that, **at the whole-myeloid depth-adjusted layer**, MMP9 retained a clearer single-gene signal than ACP5 (Supplementary Table S2). The two statements are compatible, and their coexistence is itself an instance of the paper's theme:

    whole-myeloid adjusted layer  ≠  within-TREM2 state-restricted layer

The manuscript should therefore state which layer it means, rather than treating the single-gene pattern as a construct-level property.

## 5. Claim-language audit

126 pattern hits across both documents; **22 in the main text**. Every main-text hit is correctly bounded:

| Term | n | Usage found |
|---|---|---|
| `demonstrated` | 4 | attribution to published work, or a future requirement ("so that concordance is demonstrated rather than assumed") |
| `validation` / `validate` / `validated` | 10 | general concept, author-contribution list, or **negated** ("not to validate", "does not validate", "not a validated protein-positive phenotype") |
| `excluded` / `exclude` | 3 | statistical usage ("the pooled estimate excluded zero") or negated ("no sample was excluded on the basis of target detectability", "cannot exclude dataset overlap") |

| `qualification` | 1 | future requirement ("paired qualification against the intended clinical assay") |
| `uninformative` | 1 | the manuscript's own operational definition |

**`mechanism` and `mechanistic` do not occur in the main text at all.** The Discussion reaches the same place with "explanation", "reading" and "interpretation". That is unusually disciplined and should be preserved.

**The single over-reaching phrase** is `prespecified analytical benchmark`, in two places:

- Abstract, L43: "…crossed the **prespecified** analytical benchmark (r=0.132, 95% CI −0.110 to 0.360)."
- Cover letter, L17: "…crossed the **prespecified** analytical benchmark."

Methods L70 states the opposite chronology: the r=0.20 threshold was "formalised on 10 August 2026, **after the primary estimates had been generated** but before the demonstration analyses were run". The same Methods section already uses the correct word: "**operational** bulk decision thresholds". L98 correctly writes "These analyses were **not** prespecified confirmatory tests."

The fix is one word in two places, and it is the only place in the manuscript where the abstract contradicts the Methods.

## 6. Wording to tighten in the manuscript

Ordered by benefit per unit of risk.

| # | Location | Current | Change to | Why |

| 1 | Abstract L43; cover letter L17 | "prespecified analytical benchmark" | "**operational** analytical benchmark" | Only internal contradiction in the paper; Methods already uses the correct word |
| 2 | L93 | "a pre-existing algorithmic nine-class stratification" | "a deterministic marker-module stratification … defined before the cross-scale analysis and containing neither the target nor the outcome genes" | Prevents a wrong reading that the subtype came from the source study |
| 3 | L236 | "MMP9 retained the clearer depth-adjusted single-gene signal while ACP5 attenuated" | add "**at the whole-myeloid layer**"; note that within TREM2_TAM both genes are positive and statistically indistinguishable | Prevents a layer-transfer error; the added note is favourable to the paper |
| 4 | L238 | "Treatment status was not consistently available across the public single-cell and bulk sources" | "Treatment status is documented for the single-cell series (17 treatment-naïve samples and 3 post-treatment metastatic biopsies) but not consistently for the bulk cohorts" | Turns an imprecise disclaimer into a specific fact |

Items 1, 2 and 4 are pure precision. Item 3 is precision that also removes an available criticism. Item 5 is a new disclosure sentence.
